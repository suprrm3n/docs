# Desktop app and offline DMs

The Tavrn desktop app adds native window controls, optional launch at sign-in, update checks, and limited offline access for recent direct messages.

## Launch Tavrn at sign-in

Open **Settings → System Notifications** and turn on **Launch at startup**. Tavrn will open quietly in the system tray when you sign in to your computer. You can turn this off at any time from the same setting.

## Minimize and close to tray

The desktop title bar has controls to minimize, maximize or restore, and close the window. Closing the window can keep Tavrn running in the system tray so you can still receive notifications. Open Tavrn again from the tray icon when you are ready to return.

## Updates

The desktop app checks for updates when it starts. When an update is ready, Tavrn shows an update prompt so you can install it.

## Read recent DMs offline

Tavrn stores up to 200 recent messages for each direct-message conversation you open on that device. When you lose your connection, you can still read and search those cached messages.

## Send a text DM while offline

Text-only direct messages can wait in an outbox when you are offline. Tavrn sends them automatically when your connection returns.

<Warning>
  Offline sending is limited to text-only direct messages. Attachments and room messages need an active connection and are not queued.
</Warning>

## Privacy of offline data

The offline cache is stored on the device where you use Tavrn. Sign out of shared computers and keep your operating-system account protected.
